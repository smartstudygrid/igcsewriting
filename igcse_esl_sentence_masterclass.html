Mastering High-Mark Sentence Variety

In IGCSE ESL writing tasks (Email, Article, Report, Review), examiners award up to 15 marks per task: 9 marks for Language (range, accuracy, sentence variety, complex structures) and 6 marks for Content (fulfilling all prompt points, register, tone, audience).

Language (9 Marks): Tenses, Conditionals, Connectives, Clause Variety Content (6 Marks): Task Completion, Relevance, Register & Format

1 Simple & Compound Sentences

Foundation

Use simple sentences for strong impact and compound sentences to join two equal main ideas.

Simple Sentence:

"The school organized an annual career fair."

Compound (FANBOYS):

"The event was well-planned, yet many students arrived late."

Connectives: For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So

2 Complex & Compound-Complex

Top Band Mark

Shows relationships like cause, contrast, time, and condition with subordinating clauses.

Complex (Subordinator):

"Although the rain was heavy, we continued our tour."

Compound-Complex:

"Because the hall was overcrowded, many parents left early, and the speakers had to shorten their presentations."

The Conditionals Matrix for Writing Tasks

Essential for Opinions & Recommendations
Zero Conditional Facts

If + Present, Present

"If schools promote recycling, waste decreases."

Best for: Reports / Articles
1st Conditional Real Future

If + Present, Will + Verb

"If you visit me next month, we will explore the coast."

Best for: Emails / Suggestions
2nd Conditional Hypothetical

If + Past Simple, Would + Verb

"If the library had more computers, students would study here more."

Best for: Reports / Articles
3rd Conditional Past Regret

If + Past Perfect, Would Have + V3

"If the organizers had provided maps, visitors would not have lost their way."

Best for: Reviews / Reports

The 4 IGCSE Genres & Target Grammar Checklist

Genre Tone & Style Key Target Tenses Signature Sentence Structures
Informal Email Friendly, lively, conversational Present Continuous, Past Simple, Present Perfect 2nd Conditionals (invitations), Informal Relative Clauses ("the guy who...")
Article Engaging, expressive, persuasive Present Simple, Present Continuous Participle Clauses ("Having spent..."), Rhetorical Questions, 1st Conditionals
Formal Report Objective, factual, professional Past Simple (findings), Present Simple (facts) Passive Voice ("It was observed that..."), Subordinate clause of purpose ("In order to...")

Section 2: Situation & Word Bank Drills

Construct sentences matching the exact scenario, using the word bank and required grammar constraints.

Target Time: 30 Mins • 8 Tasks
Informal Email • Drill 1 Past Simple + 2nd Conditional

Situation: Missed Meetup & Invitation

Apologize to your friend for missing their birthday party last week, and invite them to visit you next weekend instead.

Word Bank:

unavoidable unfortunate reschedule weekend delighted

Grammar Targets:

  • 1x Complex sentence using because or due to
  • 1x 2nd Conditional sentence (If + past simple, would + verb)
Model Solution:

"I was so sorry that I missed your birthday party due to an unavoidable family emergency. If you came over to my house next weekend to reschedule, I would be absolutely delighted to make it up to you!"

Informal Email • Drill 2 Relative Clause + 1st Conditional

Situation: Recommending a New Hobby

Write to a classmate suggesting they join the new school photography club with you.

Word Bank:

passion instructor equipment exhibition improve

Grammar Targets:

  • 1x Relative Clause using who or which
  • 1x 1st Conditional (If + present simple, will + verb)
Model Solution:

"Our new photography instructor, who has a real passion for teaching, provides amazing equipment. If you join the club with me, your camera skills will improve rapidly before the end-of-year exhibition."

School Article • Drill 3 Participle Clause + Zero Conditional

Situation: Smartphone Usage in School

Write an opening for a school magazine article discussing how continuous notifications disrupt teenagers' focus during study time.

Word Bank:

distraction concentration constantly academic detrimental

Grammar Targets:

  • 1x Participle Clause sentence starting with an -ing verb (e.g., "Facing endless...")
  • 1x Zero Conditional (If + present simple, present simple)
Model Solution:

"Facing constantly buzzing notifications on their phones, students frequently suffer from a loss of deep concentration. If teenagers allow digital distraction during revision, their academic performance suffers greatly."

School Article • Drill 4 Compound-Complex Sentence

Situation: Promoting Green Campus Initiatives

Write a persuasive conclusion for an article urging your school to eliminate single-use plastics from the canteen.

Word Bank:

eliminate sustainability initiative canteen inspire

Grammar Targets:

  • 1x Compound-Complex sentence using although + coordinating conjunction (and / but)
  • Modal verb of recommendation (should / ought to)
Model Solution:

"Although replacing plastic containers in the canteen may require an initial budget, this eco-friendly initiative will promote long-term sustainability, and our student council should take immediate action to inspire other schools."

Formal Report • Drill 5 Passive Voice + In Order To

Situation: School Library Upgrade

Report on the findings regarding outdated books and lack of silent study rooms in the library.

Word Bank:

inspected outdated resources insufficient allocated

Grammar Targets:

  • 1x Formal Passive Voice sentence ("It was observed that..." / "Funds were...")
  • 1x Subordinate clause of purpose using in order to or so that
Model Solution:

"During the inspection, it was noted that several reference resources were highly outdated. In order to resolve this insufficient provision, additional funds must be allocated for digital upgrading."

Formal Report • Drill 6 Formal Complex + 2nd Conditional

Situation: Sports Facilities Assessment

Provide a recommendation for improving the school's sports changing rooms and football pitch.

Word Bank:

renovation participation inadequate substantially recommend

Grammar Targets:

  • 1x Complex sentence starting with Whereas or While
  • 1x Formal 2nd Conditional recommendation (If the management were to... student participation would...)
Model Solution:

"While current outdoor pitches are well-maintained, indoor changing facilities remain strictly inadequate. If school authorities were to authorize an immediate renovation, student participation in extra-curricular sports would increase substantially."

Restaurant Review • Drill 7 3rd Conditional + Relative Clause

Situation: Evaluating Service & Atmosphere

Review a recently opened Italian cafe where the food was great but service was painfully slow.

Word Bank:

atmosphere exquisite sluggish disappointing flawless

Grammar Targets:

  • 1x Non-defining Relative Clause using , which ... ,
  • 1x 3rd Conditional showing evaluative regret (If + past perfect, would have + V3)
Model Solution:

"The homemade pasta, which was served with fresh herbs, tasted absolutely exquisite in a vibrant atmosphere. However, if the sluggish staff had been faster, our overall dining experience would have been flawless."

Event Review • Drill 8 Past Perfect + Despite

Situation: School Drama Festival

Review a drama performance held at your school last week, highlighting the actors' effort despite technical glitches.

Word Bank:

anticipated technical glitch performance captivate

Grammar Targets:

  • 1x Sentence using Past Perfect (had + V3) to show prior preparation
  • 1x Sentence starting with Despite + Noun/-ing
Model Solution:

"The drama club had rehearsed for months for this eagerly anticipated play. Despite minor technical glitches with the sound equipment, the lead actors managed to captivate the audience with an outstanding performance."
