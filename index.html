Mastering High-Mark Sentence Variety

In IGCSE ESL writing tasks (Email, Article, Report, Review), examiners award up to 15 marks per task: 9 marks for Language (range, accuracy, sentence variety, complex structures) and 6 marks for Content (fulfilling all prompt points, register, tone, audience).

1 Simple & Compound Sentences

Foundation

Use simple sentences for strong impact and compound sentences to join two equal main ideas using FANBOYS.

Compound (FANBOYS):

"The event was well-planned, yet many students arrived late."

2 Complex & Compound-Complex

Top Band Mark

Shows relationships like cause, contrast, time, and condition with subordinating clauses.

Compound-Complex:

"Because the hall was overcrowded, many parents left early, and the speakers had to shorten their presentations."

Conditionals Matrix for IGCSE ESL

Zero Conditional

If + Present, Present

"If schools promote recycling, waste decreases."

1st Conditional

If + Present, Will + V1

"If you visit me, we will explore the coast."

2nd Conditional

If + Past, Would + V1

"If we had more computers, students would study more."

3rd Conditional

If + Past Perf, Would Have + V3

"If they had brought maps, they would not have got lost."
